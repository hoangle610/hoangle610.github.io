FAQ

Why does it need access to all websites?

Popups can come from any site, so the blocker has to run everywhere. It only intercepts window.open and reads nothing else. There is no server; nothing leaves your browser.

Will it break my bank or work tools?

Sign-in and payment flows are explicitly allowed, and 1000+ popular domains are pre-whitelisted. If something still breaks, press "Site broken? Fix it".

What happens when the trial ends?

Popup blocking stays free forever. Pro modules switch off until you enter a license key. Your settings are kept.

How is this different from uBlock Origin?

uBlock Origin is a full content blocker that Chrome is phasing out with Manifest V2. Popup Terminator is focused on popups and overlays, built on Manifest V3, and designed to coexist with any ad blocker you already use.
