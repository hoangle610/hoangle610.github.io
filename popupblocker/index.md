# Privacy Policy for Popup Blocker Pro

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026

Popup Blocker Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We strongly believe in data minimization. Popup Blocker Pro operates entirely locally on your device. 
- **No Browsing History:** We do not track, collect, or transmit your web browsing history.
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party tracking tools or analytics inside the extension.

## 2. Local Storage
The extension uses your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) exclusively to save:
- Your user preferences (e.g., enable/disable toggles).
- Your whitelisted websites.
- Your license key state (for Pro users).
This data never leaves your device unless you actively synchronize it via your own Google Chrome profile sync.

## 3. License Validation (Pro Version)
If you upgrade to the Pro version, you will be required to enter a License Key. 
When validating this key, the extension makes a secure, one-time network request to our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy) solely to verify the validity of the key. No personal data or browsing data is transmitted during this process.

## 4. Payment Processing
All payments are handled securely by our third-party payment processor (Lemon Squeezy) on their securely hosted checkout pages. The extension itself does not collect, process, or store your credit card information or financial data.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official GitHub repository.
